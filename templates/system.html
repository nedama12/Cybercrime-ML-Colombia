Every account is created as a citizen. Roles are granted only by an administrator (or by the server configuration), and every operational route checks the role on the server; a user without permission receives "Access denied" (HTTP 403).

{% endcall %} {% call fold('Business processes', kicker='What the system manages', open=true) %}
  1. P1 Account and access management. Registration, login, logout, password change, account deletion, roles and deactivation.
  2. P2 Incident reporting and classification. The citizen reports in three steps; the classifier assigns the legal category, confidence and priority; the platform shows first steps and official channels.
  3. P3 Review and closing. Analysts work the queue by priority, confirm or correct the category, add a note and close the report; every change is kept in the report history.
  4. P4 Feedback and incident handling. Users send requests (wrong result, technical problem, data request, suggestion); staff answer and resolve them.
  5. P5 Analysis and indicators. Business indicators of the reports and territorial and temporal analysis of the official records.
  6. P6 Model operation. Inference history, model registry, monitoring of errors, response times, agreement and drift, and operational reviews.
{% endcall %} {% call fold('Business rules', kicker='Rules enforced by the server') %}
  • Report statuses: {% for key, label in statuses.items() %}{{ label }}{% if not loop.last %} → {% endif %}{% endfor %}. A report can be closed only with a confirmed category.
  • The citizen can edit or delete a report only while it is "Received"; after that it is kept unchanged for the audit trail.
  • If the description, channel or affected items change, the report is classified again and the new inference is recorded.
  • Priority: High when the classifier confidence is below {{ '%.0f'|format((report_info.low_confidence_threshold|default(0.45)) * 100) }} %, the citizen disagrees with the result, the loss is COP 1,000,000 or more, or a company system was affected; Medium when money or an account was affected; otherwise Normal.
  • Reports with low confidence show "Needs analyst review" instead of a category.
  • Each citizen sees only their own reports and requests; analysts see all reports but the CSV export contains no names, emails or free text.
  • A support request cannot be resolved without an answer for the user. Messages that contain a password are rejected.
  • Administrators cannot change their own role and the last administrator cannot delete their account. Every administrative action is written to the audit log.
  • Login: 5 failed attempts for one email from one address within 10 minutes, or 20 from one address, block new attempts for 2 minutes.

{% endcall %} {% call fold('Where machine learning contributes', kicker='Decisions supported') %}
  • Category of the incident. The classifier ({{ report_info.algorithm|default('')|replace('_',' ')|title }}, version {{ report_info.version|default('-') }}) turns the citizen's description, channel and affected items into one of eight legal categories. The category decides the first steps and the official channels shown to the citizen.
  • Who decides. When the confidence is low the platform does not show a category: the report goes to the top of the analyst queue. The analyst always confirms or corrects the category before closing.
  • Priority of the queue. Low confidence and citizen disagreement raise the priority, so human attention goes where the model is least reliable.
  • Learning loop. Confirmed categories are the real outcomes: they measure the model in production and become training data for the next version.
