Interpretation

What these outputs mean

  • Counts describe recorded processes in the source, not the incidence or prevalence of all cybercrime.
  • Territorial and temporal patterns can reflect reporting, registration, and coverage changes.
  • Annual forecasts extrapolate historical counts; they are not risk scores, causal explanations, or guarantees.
  • The 2026 partial year is excluded from forecasts and should not be compared directly with complete years.
  • Population rates are available only for report years with a stored denominator and matched territory. The existing population source workbook is absent, so the processed municipal crosswalk still needs independent review.
