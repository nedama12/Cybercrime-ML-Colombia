01 / The research

Making sense of a changing landscape.

Cybercrime Analysis and Prediction in Colombia Using Machine Learning

Problem statement & justification

Cybercrime affects individuals, organizations, and institutions, while reported incidents offer a valuable starting point for understanding its reach. Analyzing available cybercrime data in Colombia can help identify geographic and temporal patterns, organize evidence, and frame questions for further research. A careful, data-informed approach can support academic understanding of this evolving issue.

◎
General objective

Analyze available data on cybercrime in Colombia using Machine Learning techniques to explore patterns and assess analytical possibilities.
