01 / The problem

Understanding a changing landscape.

Many victims never report a cybercrime because they do not know what it is called or where to start.

{% call fold('What does this platform do?', kicker='Purpose', open=true) %}

It turns a short description written by the victim into the legal category of the incident (Law 1273 of 2009), gives practical first steps and keeps the report so an analyst can review it.

{% endcall %} {% call fold('Why is your report important?', kicker='Your contribution') %}

Official statistics count cases, but they do not say how people are attacked. Your report adds the channel, what was affected and your own words. Confirmed reports also train the classifier, so it gets better for the next person.

{% endcall %} {% call fold('Who built it?', kicker='Team') %}

A three-member academic team: Data Engineering (Attorney General's Office and DANE data), Model Engineering (Decision Tree, Random Forest and Logistic Regression) and Application Engineering (accounts, reports, review workflow and deployment).

{% endcall %}
