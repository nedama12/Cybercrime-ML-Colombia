Conclusion

Exploratory, not operational

The models struggle to separate categories using the available features. Logistic Regression better recovers small categories at the cost of lower precision. Future validation requires incident-level examples and temporal or group-based evaluation.

Limitations

  • Aggregated data, not independent records of individual incidents.
  • Imbalanced distribution and small minority classes.
  • Label created using an approximate mapping of legal articles.
  • Logistic Regression convergence warning.
  • The random split does not measure generalization to new territories or periods.
  • The phishing dataset described in README is not included.
